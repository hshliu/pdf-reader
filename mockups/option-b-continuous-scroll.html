第一章 引言

In recent years, the field of artificial intelligence has witnessed remarkable advances. From natural language processing to computer vision, deep learning models have achieved unprecedented performance across a wide range of tasks.

These developments have been driven by three key factors: the availability of large-scale datasets, the increasing computational power of modern hardware, and algorithmic innovations.

— 第 4 页 —

However, significant challenges remain. The interpretability of deep neural networks continues to be a major concern, particularly in high-stakes applications such as healthcare and autonomous driving.

Furthermore, the environmental impact of training large models has come under scrutiny. Studies have shown that the carbon footprint of training a single large transformer model can be substantial.

— 第 5 页 —

In this work, we propose a novel framework that addresses both the interpretability and efficiency challenges. Our approach combines attention mechanisms with sparse architectures.

The key insight is that by constraining the attention patterns to follow interpretable structures, we can achieve both high accuracy and explainability.
